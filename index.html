About the Studio

We Capture More
Than Pictures.

PaLupe Photography is a photography studio in Egbeda, Lagos. We help clients preserve important moments through carefully composed, high-quality photography — the people you love, the milestones you worked for, and the everyday beauty in between.

Every session is guided with patience: thoughtful lighting, honest direction, and attention to detail, so you feel comfortable and look your best. Whether in our studio or on location, we focus on images you will be proud to keep, print and share.
